#### 0.1 Book Thieves, Big Teeth, and Kids with Guns

**Date:** 7/12/2026
**Party:** [[Players/Shelf Books\|Shelf]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Lio\|Lio]]
<!--private-->
**Session:** [[Umm/2H Scrollish Serial Killers/Sessions/0A Prelude/260712 Pregic Hating House\|260712 Pregic Hating House]]
<!--endprivate-->

The party begins in [[Umm/Locations/Book's Books\|Book's Books]], a bookstore and artifact shop in the Eastern quiet part of the [[Umm/Locations/Park of Shoe\|Park of Shoe]], just on the cusp of the [[Shady Side\|Shady Side]].  We're introduced to the party starting with the shop's owner, [[Players/Shelf Books\|Shelf Books]], an Elderly, well-ish-to-do Tortle with a banjo and hand painted shell. 

Idling wandering the store, we also find [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]], a 10 year old boy in too big Merlin-wizard style navy blue robes whose been grounded by his master [[Umm/Characters/Priscilla\|Priscilla]]. 

[[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]], the clockwork rabbit is seen flipping through cook books with torn out pages looking his next recipe to no avail. 

[[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]], an [[Umm/Character Creation/Species/Enoki\|Enoki]] looking like a cross between a yellow-green slime mold and short Ent creature, silently admires the mold in the humid shop. 

And finally [[Umm/Characters/Players/Lio\|Lio]], a heterochromatic Tiefling with matching horns, who is slammed against the wall by [[Drexis\|Drexis]], a red Dragonborn on the look out for danger, whose decided to assert his dominance on the most pitiful looking of the party.

[[Umm/Characters/Players/Lio\|Lio]] takes this opportunity to pick his pockets for 7gp, before his quickly distracted by a gilded book elsewhere in the shop. [[Drexis\|Drexis]] is revealed to be the body guard of [[Umm/Characters/Tasis Von Luft\|Tasis Von Luft]] who enters with his servant girl and apprentice [[Umm/Characters/Bright Von Luft\|Bright Von Luft]] whose carefully balancing books. He presses the party, primarily [[Players/Shelf Books\|Shelf Books]], for an entrance to a rumored secret artifact basement, and right before he's let in through the [[Players/Shelf Books\|Shelf]]'s airlock passage, a young kid in a trench grabs a few books of the shelfs and from [[Umm/Characters/Bright Von Luft\|Bright]]'s stack, and bolts out the door to joins his friends.

[[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]] cast entangle pinning all but the robed girl. A couple books, but not all were recovered, as the girl runs off. [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter]]'s cast is noticed by [[Chomper\|Chomper]] a mysterious character identified as some sort of law enforcement. He lightly harangues the party before, [[Umm/Characters/Tasis Von Luft\|Tasis]] exits the shop and says he'll pay anything for the return of those last books.

[[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]]'s able to locate that the kids who got a way, and [[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]] good ears and [[Umm/Characters/Players/Lio\|Lio]]'s quick bird-based thinking. They confront them, and one has a gun! A short fight ensues before a gray goozer, fire weird, and ice weird, show up with guns, grab the books, and run. The ice weird is pelted by a barrage of spells from the party and falls over dead, with the ooze grabbing the books and running off. But the party is able to sleep the Fire weird time him and up and begin his interrogation. 

But the last books, gang members, and kids have run off.

A couple questions remain?
- Why did that kid have a gun? Where did he even get it?
- Why was that girl wearing robes of the [[Umm/Lore/Organizations/House of Hurricanes\|Seamanite Assembly]]?
- The gang Jack's? But whose Jack and why does here care about getting these books?
- Are you getting paid?
- What's the deal with [[Chomper\|Chomper]]? Why didn't he do anything about the kids or the books?

#### 0.3 Eliot's Mysterious Scrollish Manufactory

**Date:** 8/6/2026
**Party:** [[Read Books\|Read Books]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]]
<!--private-->
**Session:** [[Umm/2H Scrollish Serial Killers/Sessions/0A Prelude/260740B Manufacturers Scrollsa Missing\|Manufactuer's Scrolls'a Missing]]
<!--endprivate-->

After [[Chomper\|Chomper]]'s dinner quest was failed, he returned with another: journey to the the industrial side of town, the [[Business Block\|Business Block]], to assist the [[Freland\|Frelish]] entrepreneur [[Elliot Morehell\|Elliot Morehell]], in discovering the location of four missing persons and some odd missing items. The result was a fireball casting one-eyed monster lying on a pile of ancient artifacts surrounded by a spattering of four different bodies.

The session begins with Elliot's introduction to the mystery. Aelio, Rulfim, Elial, and Lacey where four employees of the experimental, specially sanctioned scroll mill who had gone missing in the past few months. Several clues turned up during the course of the investigation: Aelio worked across all positions, notably also as a scroll tester. Elial worked on the third floor as an artificer and relic procurer. Rulfim worked the second as a skilled scribe, and finally, Lacey on the bottom as an unskilled scribe. By visiting the third floor medic, they found out Aelio had skipped past required medical assessments before disappearing. By talking to the secretary, a wispy whited hair soft-spoken man, [[Read Books\|Read Books]] found that Rulfim and Elial left after hours while Aelio and Lacey never check out on their last day. By talking to a parrot wildling name Crickets on the first floor, [[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]] and [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]] found Lacey had a reputation for digging through the trash and stealing scrolls. Crickets also alluded to reports from [[Freland\|Freland]] about mysterious one-eyed beast showing up around scroll mills, a fact [[Elliot Morehell\|Elliot]], a Freelander himself, somewhat corroborated. 

During much of this investigation, [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]] explored the storage closet freely through invisibility locating a mysterious shining helmet which he abruptly tossed out a window in fear of getting caught. In doing so, he noticed another clue, another gray-purple spot of sludge in the back alley. However, the helmet was eventually returned by a blonde twin-braided dwarf to the boss [[Elliot Morehell\|Elliot Morehell]], and for his mysterious cunning, [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]] was offered a vague weekend position among [[Elliot Morehell\|Morehell]]'s staff.

The party ended up in said alley, discovering the sludge was melted human flesh. Noticing the upturned sewer grate, they journeyed downwards to discover a bulging one-eyed beast cowering in the dark on a pile of magical items, but the fight ended swiftly. With no seriously injured - with the exception of a slightly charred [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]] of course.
