# Scrollish Serial Slaughters

## Session Recaps

### Part 1

#### Book Thieves

**Date:** 7/12/2026

The party begins in [[Book's Books\|Book's Books]], a bookstore and artifact shop in the Eastern quiet part of the [[Umm/Locations/Park of Shoe\|Park of Shoe]], just on the cusp of the [[Shady Side\|Shady Side]].  We're introduced to the party starting with the shop's owner, [[Umm/2H Scrollish Serial Killers/Shelf Books\|Shelf Books]], an Elderly, well-ish-to-do Tortle with a banjo and hand painted shell. 

Idling wandering the store, we also find [[Timbo\|Timbo]], a 10 year old boy in too big Merlin-wizard style navy blue robes whose been grounded by his master [[Priscilla\|Priscilla]]. 

[[Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]], the clockwork rabbit is seen flipping through cook books with torn out pages looking his next recipe to no avail. 

[[Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]], an [[Umm/Character Creation/Species/Enoki\|Enoki]] looking like a cross between a yellow-green slime mold and short Ent creature, silently admires the mold in the humid shop. 

And finally [[Lio\|Lio]], a heterochromatic Tiefling with matching horns, who is slammed against the wall by [[Drexis\|Drexis]], a red Dragonborn on the look out for danger, whose decided to assert his dominance on the most pitiful looking of the party.
