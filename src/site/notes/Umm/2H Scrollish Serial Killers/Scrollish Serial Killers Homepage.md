We're introduced to the party starting with the shop's owner, [[Players/Shelf Books\|Shelf Books]], an Elderly, well-ish-to-do Tortle with a banjo and hand painted shell. 

Idling wandering the store, we also find [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]], a 10 year old boy in too big Merlin-wizard style navy blue robes whose been grounded by his master [[Umm/Characters/Priscilla\|Priscilla]].

[[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]], an [[Umm/Character Creation/Species/Enoki\|Enoki]] looking like a cross between a yellow-green slime mold and short Ent creature, silently admires the mold in the humid shop.

[[Umm/Characters/Players/Lio\|Lio]] takes this opportunity to pick his pockets for 7gp, before his quickly distracted by a gilded book elsewhere in the shop. [[Drexis\|Drexis]] is revealed to be the body guard of [[Umm/Characters/Tasis Von Luft\|Tasis Von Luft]] who enters with his servant girl and apprentice [[Umm/Characters/Bright Von Luft\|Bright Von Luft]] whose carefully balancing books. He presses the party, primarily [[Players/Shelf Books\|Shelf Books]], for an entrance to a rumored secret artifact basement, and right before he's let in through the [[Players/Shelf Books\|Shelf]]'s airlock passage, a young kid in a trench grabs a few books of the shelfs and from [[Umm/Characters/Bright Von Luft\|Bright]]'s stack, and bolts out the door to joins his friends.

[[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]] cast entangle pinning all but the robed girl. A couple books, but not all were recovered, as the girl runs off. [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter]]'s cast is noticed by [[Chomper\|Chomper]] a mysterious character identified as some sort of law enforcement. He lightly harangues the party before, [[Umm/Characters/Tasis Von Luft\|Tasis]] exits the shop and says he'll pay anything for the return of those last books.

[[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]]'s able to locate that the kids who got a way, and [[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]] good ears and [[Umm/Characters/Players/Lio\|Lio]]'s quick bird-based thinking. They confront them, and one has a gun! A short fight ensues before a gray goozer, fire weird, and ice weird, show up with guns, grab the books, and run. The ice weird is pelted by a barrage of spells from the party and falls over dead, with the ooze grabbing the books and running off. But the party is able to sleep the Fire weird time him and up and begin his interrogation. 

But the last books, gang members, and kids have run off.

#### 0.3 Eliot's Mysterious Scrollish Manufactory

**Date:** 8/6/2026
**Party:** [[Umm/2H Scrollish Serial Killers/Read Books\|Read Books]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]]
<!--private-->
**Session:** [[Umm/2H Scrollish Serial Killers/Sessions/0A Prelude/260740B Manufacturers Scrollsa Missing\|Manufactuer's Scrolls'a Missing]]
<!--endprivate-->

After [[Chomper\|Chomper]]'s dinner quest was failed, he returned with another: journey to the the industrial side of town, the [[Business Block\|Business Block]], to assist the [[Freland\|Frelish]] entrepreneur [[Elliot Morehell\|Elliot Morehell]], in discovering the location of four missing persons and some odd missing items. The result was a fireball casting one-eyed monster lying on a pile of ancient artifacts surrounded by a spattering of four different bodies.

The session begins with Elliot's introduction to the mystery. Aelio, Rulfim, Elial, and Lacey where four employees of the experimental, specially sanctioned scroll mill who had gone missing in the past few months. Several clues turned up during the course of the investigation: Aelio worked across all positions, notably also as a scroll tester. Elial worked on the third floor as an artificer and relic procurer. Rulfim worked the second as a skilled scribe, and finally, Lacey on the bottom as an unskilled scribe. By visiting the third floor medic, they found out Aelio had skipped past required medical assessments before disappearing. By talking to the secretary, a wispy whited hair soft-spoken man, [[Umm/2H Scrollish Serial Killers/Read Books\|Read Books]] found that Rulfim and Elial left after hours while Aelio and Lacey never check out on their last day. By talking to a parrot wildling name Crickets on the first floor, [[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]] and [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]] found Lacey had a reputation for digging through the trash and stealing scrolls. Crickets also alluded to reports from [[Freland\|Freland]] about mysterious one-eyed beast showing up around scroll mills, a fact [[Elliot Morehell\|Elliot]], a Freelander himself, somewhat corroborated. 

During much of this investigation, [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]] explored the storage closet freely through invisibility locating a mysterious shining helmet which he abruptly tossed out a window in fear of getting caught. In doing so, he noticed another clue, another gray-purple spot of sludge in the back alley. However, the helmet was eventually returned by a blonde twin-braided dwarf to the boss [[Elliot Morehell\|Elliot Morehell]], and for his mysterious cunning, [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]] was offered a vague weekend position among [[Elliot Morehell\|Morehell]]'s staff.

The party ended up in said alley, discovering the sludge was melted human flesh. Noticing the upturned sewer grate, they journeyed downwards to discover a bulging one-eyed beast cowering in the dark on a pile of magical items, but the fight ended swiftly. With no seriously injured - with the exception of a slightly charred [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]] of course.

#### 1.3 Three Visits

**Date:** 10/4/2026
**Party:** [[Umm/2H Scrollish Serial Killers/Read Books\|Read Books]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]], [[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]], [[Mercutio\|Mercutio]]
<!--private-->
**Session:** [[Umm/2H Scrollish Serial Killers/Sessions/1A Three Pillars/261004 Three Visits\|261004 Three Visits]]
<!--endprivate-->
**TL;DR:** In this session, the party, joined by [[Chomper\|Chomper]], visited [[Patsy Puff\|Patsy Puff]], the foreign affairs merchandise administrator who may have seen the Killer, an unnamed cultist, and "Regy" Deathsong.

On First Yaday, Beo, 652 AS, the party awoke in [[Umm/Locations/Book's Books\|Book's Books]]. [[Umm/2H Scrollish Serial Killers/Read Books\|Read Books]] searched for a book containing ancient maps but found nothing. The party left the store to find [[Arren Earthsea\|Arren Earthsea]], the [[Umm/Herd of the Horse Lord\|Autarunian]] paladin, missing from protecting the shop. After pondering clues, the party set off to find [[Patsy Puff\|Patsy Puff]] and discover what she knew. But before they could do so, [[Chomper\|Chomper]] appeared. He soft launched the idea of him staying with them in the store, before saying that a cultist had been detained in the jail. The party decided to go there first. Dropping their stuff at the jail entrance, the part found the cultist pacing and mumbling in his cell "King Ao is coming. The King in Green is coming. Zefir will return..." [[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]] noticed the mumbling. The word Zefir reminded him of the word [[Za'esitor\|Za'esitor]], but he could not place it. After discussing with him for a time, he said something along the lines of meet the cult in [[Umm/Locations/Shoe\|Shoe]] "In the darkest night, in the dungeon and caverns that were here before the dust." He then handed [[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]] a small conch with a spell scroll tucked inside previously detected by [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelter from the Downpour]].

The party then went to check on "Regy" in his jail cell, erupting a series of hijinks leading to Regy in cuffs; his belongings now belong to [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|Shelly]]. After, the party headed to the nearby neighborhood of [[Hightower\|Hightower]], through its bustling streets noting the [[Eyegrapes\|Eyegrapes]] and [[Blimp Frogs\|Blimp Frogs]], before entering the inner tower and making their way to [[Patsy Puff\|Patsy Puff]]'s house. After knocking on the door, with STD, Chomper, and Regy (in cuffs) waiting outside, they entered Patsy's house. After talking with her, she revealed she didn't see anything but a smudge on a nearby building and felt a strange feeling like someone dropping a stinky egg — a sensation similar to the sleep-poad combo attempted by [[Umm/Characters/Players/Timbo\|Timbo]] and [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|STD]] earlier. About 20 minutes later she recalled a handsome, nice, shirtless man with green pants who looked like a [[Umm/Locations/Bibb\|Bibbian]] but talked like a [[Umm/Locations/Merusia\|Merusian]] and who tried talking to her about what she saw. In this talk, after a flick of the fellow's hands, she again experience a similar sensation as the sleep-PoaD combo. Wanting more information, the party searched her house and caught some of her "missing" cats; Timbo ate some soup. In frustration, [[Umm/2H Scrollish Serial Killers/Read Books\|Read]] knocked old patsy out, and the party positioned a strong drink — one of many in her cabinets — next to her. Waking her and placing her glasses on her head, she thanked [[Umm/Characters/Players/Shelter from the Downpour\|STD]] for finding her cats, paid him 10 gold, and then after some more conversation with the party, revealed no more information.

In a final act in the house, [[STD\|STD]] took the conch [[Umm/Characters/Players/Cogsworth\|Cogsworth]] received in the last session, pulled the scroll out, and read it, transforming it into a tall male Goliath with a distinctive scale tattoo covering his arms, shoulders, and face.
