# Relik, Might of Cycles

**Domain:** Life and Death
**Type:** Might (God)
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
**Symbol:** half-sun and crescent moon connect by a line
**Indicator of Patronage:** Painted skulls, barren soil, archeological artifacts
**Ideals:** All is motion, all motion is cyclical. The fallen shall rise again, and the risen shall fall.
**Avatar Appearance:** A stormy sun rise, a quiet sunset, a cloudless night, a withered blossom
**Typical Worshipers:** Prior to industrial period of the [[Umm/Lore/The History of Umm#vi. The Currendian Period\|Currendian]], Relik worship was lost to time, but religious scholars participating in experimental practices have found that Relk, thought lost to time, is currently active and somewhat reliable, offering consistent boons and cleric operations. Many in [[Umm/Locations/Shoe\|Shoe]] have begun worshiping them for a somewhat quick and easy magical payoff.
