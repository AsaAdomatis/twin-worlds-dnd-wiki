# Ninígheb, the Many Named Creatorum

Ninígheb is the first for everything in [[Umm/Lore/Umm\|Ümm]]. They are single and multiple simultaneously hence the word Creatorum, not creator or creators. They sailed across the [[Earthen Sea\|Earthen Sea]] and created [[Ümm\|Ümm]] from nothing, and left shortly after. 

**Names:** Ninígheb's many names include All-Maker(s), Alma, Ur-God, and Grand Might.
