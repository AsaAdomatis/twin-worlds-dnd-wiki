# A Bee's Tail

> I'm just a bramble-on-a-bee, lost on an empty shore. But I too am old. I've witnessed many thing here and there, and I could even say some were worth witnessing. I call my earliest memories "Tommy's Quiet Times" but other have incorrectly named it "the Era of Alma's Children." So let me spin you a tail of our earliest Mights.
> 
> It begins with a dreamer whose names are endless:  All-makers, Ur-God, Grand Might, Ninígheb  or even just Gheb. Though I'm personally found of the name Alma. Alma was the first of everything for Ümm. They sailed cross the Earthen Sea to the next available plot, and stuttering, gave it its name. They built great valleys and greater mountains and seas of glittering coral and desert of shining sand. And while fun for a short time, plate tectonics can only be so interesting. So Alma took the empty island in the north, and there, created the wonderful and wild Mungrass and Claymen. Either completely satisfied, or finding a more interesting idea, Alma sailed onward. But just before leaving, they construct a great clay pot with a portion of the mung and the men, and left a stew brewing. This stew would bear us Zerula.
> 
> Zerula was the first of the Mights and the Moshas. She is the Other's Mother. She was the only true offspring of Alma. Zerula was born to a world quite empty and unimpressive, so she sought to give it life. First she created the Moshas (Maybe you've heard them called Titans, but that name is also empty and unimpressive). The Moshas are the laws of the Thread, the forces of nature. They carry almost no sentience nor independence. The Moshas add depth, but untold years of watching them revealed recurring patterns. So, she created two Mights (Again, colloquially, a God). These Mights name's were Cicibrion and Zaesitor, brother and sister, prince and princess.
> 
> Zerula whispered to each "find your place" then disappeared into the mist of the Astral Sea. Zaesitor chose to seek other realms among the Thread and headed eastward. Cicibrion seeing nothing to the west, stayed put. He began to entertain himself. First, he traveled Ümm at all different sizes to gain some perspective. Second, he toyed with the Moshas to understand the paradigms. Then third, he began to iterate; he created more Mights. 
> 
> As a small bramble bee, I can't say I've met them all, but I can tell you the names I recall. To this point, they are the Arcmights: Roknoc, Chica, Castius, Relik, and Tusit; the Midights: Luwom, Aug, Curio, Ratio, Ashet, and Flit. And countless Impights, for which I'm too occupied to recount.
> 
> - Tom, the bramble-on-a-bee, to a prophet unlost at sea
