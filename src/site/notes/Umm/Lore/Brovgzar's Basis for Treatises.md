# Brovgzar's Treatises

> From this era, the Era of True Mights, I have learned little. My scavengers yield little, and Shoe's library, even less. However, I can recount this: There were many people, and they where held in great light by the Divine. Through them they accomplished many great things exemplified by their great works of architecture like the Mines of Mountmouth, the Abyssal City, and of import to my readers, the Plastic City. This relationship faltered after time, and the Might's smit their children mocking the the monuments built to honor their divinity.
> 
> To my treasonous colleagues: this statements are well-researched, well-supported, and all but certain.
> 
> To my sweet mother: I don't know a felly thing about what really happened.
> 
> To you, the Mutt: Continue investigation as if these monuments possess a doom curse.
> 
> - A letter from the esteemed academic of Shoe, [[Umm/Lore/Brovgzar the Barely Blue\|Brovgzar, the Barely Blue]] addressed to [[Umm/Characters/Thomas Dugal Kane\|Thomas Dugal Kane]] explaining the basis of his Treatises, later intercepted by the [[Umm/Lore/Organizations/House of Hurricanes\|House of Hurricanes]]
