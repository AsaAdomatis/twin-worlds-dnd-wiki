# Deghibabt's Invasion

About 1000 years ago, the lich [[Deghibabt\|Deghibabt]] led an invasion of the [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Kingdom of Pot]].

The Goliath druid [[Fee\|Fee]] stopped him by tearing a single mountain from the earth and raising it bodily into the sky. The cataclysm of the mountain's uprooting crushed [[Deghibabt\|Deghibabt]]'s invading army outright. That mountain, raised and never set back down, became [[Umm/Locations/Fofam\|Fofam]] — the city has always carried that name, even before it took to the sky.

In the ~1000 years since, [[Fee\|Fee]] has kept [[Umm/Locations/Fofam\|Fofam]] aloft by spending down his own immortality, acting as the city's engine rather than its ruler. That makes the city's flight a finite thing, not a permanent one.

Quiet rumors persist that [[Deghibabt\|Deghibabt]] is regaining power in the far reaches of the realm (see [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Kingdom of Pot]]).
