# Cult of the Drowned

The Cult of the Crowned is a religious confederacy in Northeast Ümm centered around the ritual called the "Drowning" or "Ashen Baptism" where cult priests baptize members — often unwilling — in secretive underground wells. They have a hedonistic caste system and are highly devout with the [[Old Hope\|Old Hope]] faiths.

The secret drowning wells where the baptism takes place hold magical power: They force the uninitiated to astral project and venture through the Lands of Ash and Shadow where the Shoom, the Shadow Sun, and other banished celestial entities and deities lives. 

This is what causes the various outcomes of the baptism. Their initiates prior mental state, the length of their stay in the land, and what they encounter while their, all contribute to this outcome.

### Zok

Zok is extremely important to understanding the Drowned. It roughly translates to "pleasurability," an individuals ability to be pleasured. This breaks down into Zokplok and Zoktla. Zokplok is capacity for pleasure — how much luxury and excess can one receive before their pleasure no longer increases. Zoktla is effectiveness of pleasure — how much does an increase in pleasure increase their enjoyment.

For example, a low Zokplok and Zoklta individual, classified usually as a Plib, are grumps: they cannot find joy in anything. A medium Zok individual could be one with high Zokplok but low Zoktla, someone who can find great joy in life, but doesn't have a refined pallet to distinguish cottons from silks. Usually they would be a Tlafoom.
### Castes

The "Drowning" has a range of outcomes with the outcome of the baptism determining caste within the confederacy. The castes are primarily based on an individuals Zok, but other factors often have sway in the final decision made by a high priest. The castes are in order from lowest to highest:

- **Draedooms**: Outsiders
- **Hraesy**: Aggressively insane
- **Plib**: Baptism-broken servants
- **Rohanochy**: Hedonist raiders
- **Tlafoom**: Devout populous
- **Devotion-Priests — Pwee**: Priests
- **Hedon-Kings — Zokzha**: Hedonistic nobility

#### Plib

The Ashen baptism broke the minds of the Plib: it showed them that it all ends into nothing. And that made the quiet grumpy. So grumpy in fact, that everything is joyless. All food, all dance, and — of great economic importance — all work is equally distasteful. The Plib do the dirty, manual labor for the Cult of the Drowned and feed the Hedon-Kings at the top.
