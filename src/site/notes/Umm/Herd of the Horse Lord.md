# Herd of the Horse Lord: Ya's Way

The Herd of the Horse Lord is the primary religion of the humans of Ümm. It follows in the wake of the great conqueror [[Umm/Character Creation/Species/Atalan Autarun\|Atalan Ya Autarun]] who defeated the Eladrin conquistadors, the [[Kaevansory\|Kaevansory]] who occupied the majority of Ümm — notably, except for the [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Kingdom of Pot]].

At the faith's core, it asserts that Ya was a Wright (Champion of the Gods) with the divine edict to unite and rule Ümm.

 Status within the faith is divined by heritage in relation to the great Horse Lord. Direct lineage, high status. No lineage, no status. To confirm these lineages, priests will perform bloodletting rituals to test for shared blood and for the presence of hereditary magic.

Functionally, the Herd is an amalgamation of all the compatible faiths of Ümm in a universalists perspective. Old Hope and Wan-Jer beliefs are synthesized with Ya respected as the center and mortal form, but not apex. This "inoffensiveness" allows anyone to theoretically convert to the Herd with fundamentally changing their current beliefs and traditions.
