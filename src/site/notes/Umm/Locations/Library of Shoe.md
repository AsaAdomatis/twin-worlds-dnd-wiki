# Library of Shoe

The grand library of the [[Umm/Locations/Shoe\|City of Shoe]] located within the walls of [[Umm/Locations/Platton\|Platton]].

## Description

### Inner Rooms

#### AAA Room

In one of the meeting spaces, shoved in the back corners of the Library of Shoe lies the [[Umm/Lore/Organizations/Assembly of Archival Ambition\|AAA]] meeting house. Initially, it appears to be a meeting space booked out for the rest of eternity, marked with strange wizard and academic paraphernalia, but more can be found on closer inspection. Under the large oak table, a trapdoor links the room to a complex network of passages to more secret rooms of the [[Umm/Lore/Organizations/Assembly of Archival Ambition\|AAA]]. The most common one to visit is one that connects to the balcony that rims the top of the library.  On most days, the members of the [[Umm/Lore/Organizations/Assembly of Archival Ambition\|AAA]] can be found up here drinking and boisterously discussing matters of academia.
