# Merusia: Iron and Dome

Merusia is one of the few human nations on [[Umm/Locations/Pan the Grand Continent\|Pan]]. It has a tumultuous history beginning with the the [[Umm/Locations/Merus\|Pilgrimages of Merus]], and mostly ending with the Merusian Conquest. 

## Geography

Merusia lies just East of the Iron lake among the shores of the [[Etrough Lake\|Etrough Lake]] in the Northern Eastern regions of the humans near [[Umm/Locations/Delvania\|Delvan]], [[Freland\|Freland]], and [[Drowntown\|Drowntown]]. It's nearby forests are filled with thickets of the pale [[Umm/Locations/Pillar Pine Trees\|Pillar Pine Trees]] and its North is marked by the distinct shape of the [[Dome\|Dome]], a perfectly round semi-sphere about as large as the city itself covered only in grass.
