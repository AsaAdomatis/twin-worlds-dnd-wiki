# Platton, the Golden Neighborhood

> The walls of Platton cast a shadow over the main stretch. These walls are the inner walls of the ancient citadel built 75ft tall with barbicans at every corner stretching even higher. Like the previous walls, vines stretch across these walls, but large fruit trees join them. Inside the these inner walls, much of the citadels original structure is retained. A beautiful smooth stone street stretches out and winds in circle and around overgrown gardens up to two white marble pillared buildings and a third castle that connects to the walls themselves. What would have been broad stretches of gardens of this inner sanctum is now dotted with beautiful Victorian-esc. manors. New stone paths  join the manors, and short stone and wrought-iron fences enclose a few yards. A large twisting cherry tree stands in the middle of the biggest garden sweetening the air with the smells of cherry.
