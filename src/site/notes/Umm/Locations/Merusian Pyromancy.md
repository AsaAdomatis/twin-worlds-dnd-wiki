# Merusian Pyromancer

> You are a veteran of the [[Umm/Locations/Merusia\|Merusian Conquest]], or you have gained the dogmatic training of the [[Umm/Locations/Merusia\|Merusian]] Mages. In the war, you would have been a terrifying light cavalrymen and an expert at [[Scrollish Magic\|Scrollish Magic]]. 

> Merusian Pyromancer rode light on fast horses and encircled enemy infantry formations and hurled explosive blasts of fire, striking fear into the unprepared.

### Background

#### 5.5e Variant

**Ability Scores:** Intelligence, Constitution, Dexterity
**Feat:** Merusian Pyromancy
**Skill Proficiencies:** Arcana and Animal Handling
**Tool Proficiency:** [Calligrapher's Supplies](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Items:Calligrapher's%20Supplies?expansion=34047#content)
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Short Sword, Calligrapher’s Supplies, Scroll Capsae, Insignia of Rank Parchment (10 sheets), Robe; 5 GP or (B) 50 GP

#### 5.0e Variant

**Skill Proficiencies:** Arcana, Animal Handling
**Tool Proficiency:** [Calligrapher's Supplies](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Items:Calligrapher's%20Supplies?expansion=34047#content), Vehicles (land)
**Language:** None
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Short Sword, Calligrapher’s Supplies, Scroll Capsae, Insignia of Rank Parchment (10 sheets), Robe; 5 GP or (B) 50 GP

##### Merusian Veteran

You are a feared class of a light cavalry from a treacherous conquest ending around 30 years ago. Merusians hold you in high regard. Other military members either treat you similarly for their respect or with great disdain to the damage your class did. Regardless, you can invoke your status to gain respect and hospitality in those who'd provide it and fear in the common folk who'd have been subject to the war.

### Feat

You gain the following benefits:

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Intelligence or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Simple Embers.** You know the [Produce Flame](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:produce-flame) cantrip. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for Merusian Pyromancy spells.

**Mastery.** This feat is repeatable. Each time you take it, including the first, choose an additional bonus below:
#### Capable Casting

**Bonus**

With 1 hour of work when you finish a long or short rest, you may create a list of potential Scrollish spell sentences to empower you fire magic: the amount is equal to 2d6 + Intelligence Modifier + Proficiency Bonus. You may maintain this list over time but the total you have written down and saved may never exceed 20.

At different levels, you learn more pyromancy-based spells. 
- You always have these spells prepared. 
- You must consume a number of Scrollish spell sentences to cast these spell as you recite each one testing is magical capability. This number is listed in the table below.
- If a spell requires components that will be consumer, you must provide those components.


| Level Acquired | Spell                                                               | Scrollish Spell Sentence Cost |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 1              | [Burning Hands](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:burning-hands)     | 5                             |
| 3              | [Continual Flame](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:continual-flame) | 7                             |
| 5              | [Fireball](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:fireball)               | 9                             |

#### Terrifying Flames

**Bonus**

When you deal a creature fire damage, you have that creature make a Wisdom saving throw against your Intelligence Spell Save DC. If they fail, they are frightened till the end of their next turn. Once you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you roll Initiative or finish a Short or Long Rest. If you've taken the Capable Casting Bonus, you may spend 2 Scrollish Spell Sentences to replenish this feature.

#### Calverous Confligration

**Bonus**

When you hit a creature with a weapon attack as part of the Attack action on your turn while you are mounted, you can cause the weapon to deal extra Fire damage to the target. The extra damage equals your Proficiency Bonus.
