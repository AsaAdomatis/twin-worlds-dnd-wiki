# Shiver, Cusp of the Artic Astrals

Shiver lies in the [[Umm/Locations/Umm Locations Index#The Shatters\|The Shatters]] and is a trade hub. It's know for its timber export and great shipbuilding prowess, with travelers often stopping for repairs. It's home to the silver hair-clad, Chilly Halflings and the fire formed [[Umm/Character Creation/Species/Weirds\|Weirds]], that decorate the local inns, taverns, and homes, deemed the Hearth Weirds.
