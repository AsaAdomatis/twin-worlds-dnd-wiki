# Forest of Giant Things

A forest of preposterous proportions the haunts the tip of the Northwest bay of [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Pot]] on the outskirts of [[Umm/Locations/Varawae\|Varawae]].

<!--private-->

## Quick Ideas

- Moonlight does something causes transmutation in a dark and corrupted way
	- Things created in the forest are not subjected to this dark magic
- Effect is tied to a school of magic, this school being transmutation
	- The [[Umm/Locations/Plastic City\|Plastic City]] is tied to illusion magic and the [[Umm/Locations/Mountmouth\|Mountmouth]] is enchantment magic
