# Fofam, City in the Sky

The capital city of the [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Kingdom of Pot]]. It rest on a floating island that soars over the bay of [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Pot]]. Many hot air balloons a tethered to each houses and rope bridges connect the free floating chunks of the island.

## History

About 1000 years ago, the lich [[Deghibabt\|Deghibabt]] led an invasion of Pot. The Goliath druid **Fee** stopped him by tearing a single mountain from the earth and raising it bodily into the sky — the cataclysm of the mountain's uprooting crushed Deghibabt's invading army outright. That mountain, raised and never set back down, is Fofam. The city has always carried the name Fofam, even before it took to the sky.

See [[Umm/Lore/Deghibabt's Invasion\|Deghibabt's Invasion]] for the fuller telling of the invasion itself.

## Governance

Fee no longer rules so much as sustains — he is the city's engine, and has spent the last 1000 years keeping Fofam aloft by spending down his own immortality. This makes Fofam's flight a finite thing, not a permanent one.

Day-to-day rule falls to his daughter **Vaya** and his old friend **Glish**. Since the Merusian Conquest, Fofam has functioned as a military base more than a civic capital. Vaya has largely stepped back from governing — she now sits with the Autarunian Council and rarely participates in Fofam's affairs, leaving Glish to run the city more or less alone.

## Economy

Fofam draws tribute from across the [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Kingdom of Pot]], not just [[Umm/Locations/Shoe\|Shoe]] — including the fisheries of [[Umm/Locations/Varawae\|Varawae]] near the [[Forest of Giant Things\|Forest of Giant Things]] and the river-fed farmland of Pot generally. Because these sources are so productive, the kingdom-wide tribute rate stays comparatively light even though Fofam depends entirely on imported food. Few of the people who pay tribute ever see the capital itself — the city's own population is small relative to the kingdom it draws from.
