# Fofam, City in the Sky

The capital city of the [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Kingdom of Pot]]. It rest on a floating island that soars over the bay of [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Pot]]. Hot air balloons tether together rocks and house, and rope bridges connect the free floating chunks. The detachments precautionary augmentations represent the growing fear in the capital that its great Wan-Jer Druid King, Fee Mountasplitter, is weakening. And soon, with his loss of magical power, the city will fall.

The city is actually split into two parts which form the slang term Fofam. Fovishin is the lower and older city, renamed for its sea facing side, and reconfigured for primarily military purposes. It's older name is Font. Famasenome is the upper new city, built on the craggy yet flat base of the old mountain top.

While the city has always functioned as more as a flying military fortress than an actual capital, the [[Umm/Locations/Merusia\|Merusian Conquest]] reminded [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Pot]] of its purpose.
## History

About 1000 years ago, the lich [[Deghibabt\|Deghibabt]] led an invasion of Pot. The Goliath druid **Fee** stopped him by tearing a single mountain from the earth and raising it bodily into the sky — the cataclysm of the mountain's uprooting crushed Deghibabt's invading army outright. That mountain, raised and never set back down, is Fofam. The city has always carried the name Fofam, even before it took to the sky.

See [[Umm/Lore/Deghibabt's Invasion\|Deghibabt's Invasion]] for the fuller telling of the invasion itself.

## Governance

Fee, the once great protector and king of Pot, no longer rules so much as he sustains the city. He is the city's engine, and he has spent the last 500 years keeping Fofam aloft by exhausting his own immortality in a permanent dance. In his absence, his now ancient gnome companion Glish governs the city.

Normally affairs would be left to the princess [[Ysmae Mountainsplitter\|Ysmae Mountainsplitter]] and her royal family, but the affairs are occupied in diplomacy. To the dismay of some [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Pottians]], [[Ysmae Mountainsplitter\|Ysmae]] now courts [[Umm/Lore/Organizations/Autarunian Concilliary\|Autarunian Council]] hoping to gain a psuedo-position, legitimize the [[Umm/Herd of the Horse Lord\|Herd of the Horse Lord]], and greater intertwine the two nations.

## Economy

Fofam draws tribute from across the [[Umm/Locations/Kingdom of Pot\|Kingdom of Pot]] including the fisheries of [[Umm/Locations/Varawae\|Varawae]] near the [[Forest of Giant Things\|Forest of Giant Things]] and the river-fed farmland of Pot. Because these sources are so productive, the kingdom-wide tribute rate has stayed comparatively light even though Fofam depends almost entirely on imported food and supplies. However, within the last two years, tax demands have been increasing drastically to the booming complaints of the populace, which tell now has rarely seen the city.
