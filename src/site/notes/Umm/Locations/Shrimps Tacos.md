Located in the [[Umm/Locations/Main Stretch\|Main Stretch]] of Shoe.
## Appearance

A small food cart on the side on the corner of one of the inner streets. It's got a cute little red and white cover. It reeks of fish.  Shrimp is the owner. He's a rather rotund elephant with a quirky voice. He's got a simple goal: cook seafood tacos. He doesn't want anymore any less. He's got a stained green and yellow apron and a tiny baseball cap.
