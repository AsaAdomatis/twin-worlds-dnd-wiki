[[Broadlands/Campaign 1/Sessions/Arch 1 - The First Dracling/Part 2 - Hell's Dragons/260410-2 The Professor and the Palace\|Session Notes]]

The players departed from the Spa in [[Broadlands/Campaign 1/Sessions/Active Encounters/Hell's Gate\|Hell's Gate]] safely and cleanly. Shortly after, they spent the night at dinner in the [[Unicorn and Hare\|Unicorn and Hare]] consuming eggs, potatoes, and mash. The awoke the next morning with time to kill, two days before the Professor's lecture and the Grand Ball at the Palace. So, they poked around. They went to the University of [[Broadlands/Campaign 1/Sessions/Active Encounters/Hell's Gate\|Hell's Gate]], which was sparse it being the weekend. [[Broadlands/Campaign 1/PC/Jerrus Famus\|Jerrus]] cast *Pass without Trace* and snuck by the attendant's desk unseen and poured ink all of the student registry before memorizing one name [[Darius Forehand\|Darius Forehand]]. Oblivious, the attendant then directed the rest of the party to each section of the University library they were looking for, but they payed special interest to the archived section.
